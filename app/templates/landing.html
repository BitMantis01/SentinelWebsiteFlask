The Critical Problem

Hospitals in the Philippines face severe neonatal nurse shortages, leading to delayed monitoring and increased risks for newborns. Every second counts— and SENTINEL bridges that gap.

Critical Nurse Shortage

Severe lack of neonatal nurses puts immense pressure on healthcare facilities, compromising infant care quality.

Unsafe Ratios

Nurse-to-infant ratios as high as 1:10, far exceeding the safe standard of 1:4.

Increased Risks

Delayed monitoring heightens dangers of SIDS, hypoxia, hypothermia, and other life-threatening complications.
